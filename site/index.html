A pocket voice companion

Hold the talk key and ask a question out loud. chirp sends the question to an AI and speaks the answer. A small pixel character, the Chirper, shows what happens: it listens, it thinks, it talks. The words show on the screen too.

Install chirp Read the README

chirp runs on two small handheld devices from LilyGO: the T-Embed CC1101 Plus (a dial and two keys) and the T-Deck (a keyboard and a trackball). It is free software under the GPL-3.0.

What you can do with it

  • Ask out loud. A fact, a recipe, a word you cannot spell. The answer comes back as speech and as text.
  • Type. On the T-Deck, type a question and push Enter.
  • Go back to a chat. With Hermes, the Chats tab resumes recent conversations.
  • Make it yours. Pick a Chirper, a theme, a font and a text size. With Venice, pick a voice.
  • Set it up from a phone. You do not need an app. chirp keeps up to 5 WiFi networks and joins the strongest one in range.

It listens only when you say so

chirp is push-to-talk. The microphone is open while you hold the talk key. When you let go, the question is sent.

Hands-free mode is off by default. It starts only when you turn it on with a quick double-tap of the talk key. Then chirp listens after each answer. It stops after 3 minutes of quiet, or when you double-tap again.

Two routes: cloud or at home

During setup you pick where the answers come from.

Cloud: Venice

Easy. You need the device, a phone and a Venice API key.

  • Chat, speech to text and the voice all come from Venice.
  • Your voice and your text go to Venice over https.
  • Venice charges credit for each answer.
  • The personality is Chirper, a friendly companion for children, built into the firmware.

Venice setup

At home: Hermes

Private. You need a Linux computer on the same WiFi.

  • Your computer runs your own Hermes Agent and the chirp speech server.
  • Your voice audio stays on your network. Speech to text and the voice run on your computer.
  • The answer comes from the Hermes model provider that you choose. The text of your question goes to that provider, unless you run the model yourself.
  • The device talks to your computer over plain HTTP on your own network. See the security notes.
  • You pay your model provider. Speech is free.

Hermes setup

Meet the Chirpers

Every agent has its own Chirper. chirp draws it from the name of the agent, so a different name gives a different character.

Pick a theme

Six colour themes, each in dark and light. Four fonts in three sizes.

Install

T-Embed CC1101 Plus

Install from your browser. No build tools. You need Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer and a USB-C cable that carries data.

Open the browser installer

Do not tick Erase device. An erase removes the saved settings.

T-Deck

The browser installer does not work on the T-Deck. Its serial loader does not answer. Flash it over USB-JTAG with OpenOCD.

T-Deck over USB-JTAG

After the install, the device starts its own WiFi network. Join it with your phone and follow the setup page. See First setup.
